About

Language learning in context

Hello! I'm Jordi Rosell. I created this app to help language learners acquire vocabulary in context.

Choose your learning language the first time you use the app. You can change it later using the learning-language control in the top bar. Supported languages include German, English, Catalan, Vietnamese, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic, Urdu, Russian, Hindi, French, Spanish and Filipino.

As a learner, you can:

  • Click on a word view, update, or add its definition, new/learning/learned status, and usage examples with images and YouTube links.
  • Use the + button to open saved texts, add one by pasting, or edit it.
  • Edit, download, or upload your vocabulary using a CSV file format. (Yes, you can load your words by simply copying and pasting them or uploading a CSV.)

Known issues:

  • Texts, vocabulary, and images are saved in this browser. They are not shared across devices.
  • This app uses a consent manager to track only users who have agreed to analytics tracking (aka. Google Analytics).
  • Examples (still) not work for mulitple words.
  • You (still) cannot upload/donlowad images for your examples. Examples (still) not work for mulitple words.

Let me know what you think. And if you want, you can contribute or open an issue.

I hope you enjoy it,
