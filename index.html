About

Language learning in context

Hello! I'm Jordi Rosell. I created this app to help language learners acquire vocabulary in context.

Choose a learning language the first time you use the app. You can change it later from the top bar. Supported languages include German, English, Catalan, Vietnamese, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic, Urdu, Russian, Hindi, French, Spanish, and Filipino.

As a learner, you can:

  • Select a word to see or edit its meaning, learning status, and examples.
  • Use + to open saved texts, paste a new text, or edit one.
  • Manage your word list using CSV: Vocabulary lets you copy, upload, edit, or download it.

Known issues:

  • Texts, vocabulary, and images are saved in this browser and are not shared across devices.
  • Analytics are collected only if you agree to tracking.
  • Examples for multiple words are not supported yet.
  • Example images cannot be uploaded or downloaded yet.

Send feedback or contribute on GitHub.

I hope you enjoy it!
